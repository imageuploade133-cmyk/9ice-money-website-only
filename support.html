Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if a transfer takes more than 10 seconds?

While 99.8% of transfers settle in under 0.5 seconds, destination bank maintenance can occasionally delay alerts. Check your transaction timeline in the 9ice Money app under "Activity". If not received after 5 minutes, our auto-reversal engine automatically returns your funds.

How do I upgrade my account limits to Tier 3?

To unlock unlimited daily transfers and international card issuance, navigate to App Profile > Verification. Upload a valid government ID (International Passport, Driver's License, or NIN) along with utility bill verification. Approval takes under 3 minutes via automated facial matching.

Are interest earnings on savings vaults taxed?

Your daily interest payouts (up to 18% per annum) are credited net of statutory withholdings where applicable. You can export official audit-ready annual tax certificates directly from the app for corporate or personal accounting.

What happens if I lose my phone or physical metal card?

You can freeze your cards immediately via web login or by calling our toll-free hotline +234 700 9ICE PAY. Your balance remains 100% safe as biometrics and 2FA PINs prevent unauthorized access.
