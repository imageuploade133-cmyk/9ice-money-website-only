Our Heritage & Purpose

Engineered for Perfection, Trusted for Scale

Established in 2020 by financial tech veterans and global security architects, 9ice Money was born out of frustration with legacy banking slowness, high transfer fees, and lack of real inflation protection.

We pioneered daily compounding high-yield savings (18% p.a.) backed by real sovereign wealth allocations, instant multi-currency settlement networks, and bespoke digital cards that effortlessly bridge local commerce and global luxury merchant acceptance.
